# War Driving

[Wardriving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wardriving) is the art of searching for Wi-Fi wireless networks as well as cell towers, usually from a moving vehicle, using a laptop or smartphone. Software for wardriving is freely available on the internet.

Warbiking, warcycling, warwalking and similar use the same approach but with other modes of transportation.

The term comes from this iconic 1983 Sci-Fi Thriller War Games.
