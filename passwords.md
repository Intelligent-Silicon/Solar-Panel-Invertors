# Change your Default Passwords

Its been said many times, change your default passwords. 

Devices, like Solar Panel Inverters, Car's, Internet of Things (IoT), [Smart Kettles](https://blog.boerzel.de/de/blog/operation-smart-kettle) basically anything with Radio Frequency communications, so mobile phone networks, Wifi , Bluetooth, [LoRa Radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LoRa) [Mesh Networking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesh_networking) can all be used to send and receive data.

Your Inverter will typically come with a bluetooth controller so that the manufacturers supplied software can be used on a computer to download data, like fault codes and generation data. 

Your Solar Panel installer wont always run through the capabilities of your inverter, leaving your private data open to anyone walking past your property with a bluetooth capable device like a mobile phone or laptop.

As the manufacturer will often provide firmware updates for the inverter, if you havent changed your password, some clever people could upload malicious firmware for the Lulz. Admittedly creating malicious firmware is a difficult job, but its not theoretically impossible, especially if gaining persistence offers some useful rewards, which will be discussed later.
